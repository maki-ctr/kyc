kycBank

Onboarding and KYC Refresh

A clickable prototype of how a Belgian retail bank could open accounts remotely for newcomers and refresh existing customers' details under the EU AML Regulation, which applies from 10 July 2027.

Two flows, one design

  • New customer: a newcomer to Belgium opens an account from their phone, sees the documents they need before any form starts, and learns in plain words which level of checks applies and what happens next.
  • Existing customer: a customer whose review is due confirms what is still right and supplies only what is missing or out of date.
